Inside the game

What you can do

Walk and explore

Roam the reading rooms and shelves in first person. Climb, jump and look around at your own pace.

Chat with others

Talk to the people in the library. Every name floats above their head with a label: Student, Staff or Admin.

Play chess

Sit at a computer and play the computer, or open an online room and share its code with a friend.

Borrow books

Pick a book from a shelf. The request is reserved on the website too, and a QR code is emailed to you for pickup.

Meet the cat

Somebody has to guard the books. Say hello and give the library cat a pet.

More on the way

New rooms, activities and events are added over time. Watch the library news for updates.
